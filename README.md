
### Hi there 👋

I'm **Mark W. Greenlee**, a professor at the Institute of Psychology, University of Regensburg, where I lead the Multisensory Research Group. My research investigates visual and vestibular perception during self-motion and visual disorders, using MRI and eye-movement measurement.

## 🔬 What I do

- 👁️ Visual & multisensory perception research (University of Regensburg — [Group page](https://www.uni-regensburg.de/humanwissenschaften/fakultaet/professuren/psychologie/ag-multisensorik/team/greenlee))
- 🧠 Vision science: self-motion perception, vestibular-visual integration, visual disorders
- 🛠️ AI-assisted development: I build research and teaching tools with AI coding assistants

## 🧪 Experiments & teaching demos

- **[online-rdk-experiment](https://github.com/markwgreenlee/online-rdk-experiment)** — browser-based random-dot motion direction discrimination (jsPsych 7, custom HTML5-canvas plugin), designed for online data collection
- **Fourier gratings** — interactive vision-science demo: a square-wave grating built from a fundamental plus odd harmonics
- **Landolt C demo** — PsychoPy gap-discrimination demo with Posner cue and backward mask
- **AI-assisted limbus eye tracking** — data and analysis (Greenlee et al., 2026)
